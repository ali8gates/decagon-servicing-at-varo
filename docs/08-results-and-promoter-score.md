## What the promoter score is

The net promoter score asks customers how likely they are to recommend Varo, from 0 to 10. People answering 9 or 10 are promoters, 0 to 6 are detractors. The score is the percent of promoters minus the percent of detractors, so it ranges from minus 100 to plus 100. It is a score, not a percentage, even though it is often said as one.

The Decagon post-chat survey measured clarity, whether the issue was resolved, whether the customer felt understood, and likelihood to recommend (App Launch and Feedback Review, Mar 10, 2026).

## How the change likely drove the score up

These are the reasons supported by the meetings. The link between each and the score is reasoned, not proven by a controlled test.

1. **More questions answered on the spot.** Card questions, the top reason customers contact Varo, went from 44% to 57% resolved without a person (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026).
2. **Plain language instead of scripts.** Customers no longer have to guess the right words (Decagon company training, Apr 3, 2026).
3. **Less waiting.** When the queue is full, Decagon keeps helping instead of leaving the customer on hold (Decagon call, Jan 20, 2026).
4. **Better handoffs.** Human agents receive a summary and history, so customers do not repeat themselves (How Decagon works walkthrough, Feb 11, 2026).
5. **Help at any hour** for common questions.

## How to present the promoter score to stakeholders

* Say "rose from about 40 to 50 to over 80," not "percent."
* Before a broad share, attach the survey dashboard and state the time period, the number of responses, and whether it measures the chat experience or Varo overall.
* Note other things that changed in the same period, such as app releases or product changes, so no one can say the gain is overstated.
* Pair it with the card resolution number, which has a clear internal source.

## Honest caveats

* Resolution was behind Lex early on (43% vs. 55%) before account actions were added (Decagon company training, Apr 3, 2026). This is normal for a staged launch and worth saying openly.
* Card resolution was leveling off by July, near what standard answers alone can achieve. The next jump depends on letting Decagon take card actions directly (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026).
* No measured cost per chat, total staff hours saved, or overall satisfaction score was found in the meetings.
