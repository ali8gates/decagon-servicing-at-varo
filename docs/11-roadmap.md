# What comes next

From the July 2026 planning review (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026). Decagon's third quarter roadmap has nine to ten items. The top three are:

| Priority | What it is | Why | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Card management | Let customers activate or freeze a card inside the chat | "I need help with my card" is the top contact reason, and resolution has leveled off at 57% | Fewer manual card requests |
| 2. Dispute intake | Collect dispute details automatically | Manual intake is slow and costly | About 40% lower cost per dispute |
| 3. Login support | Help customers who cannot sign in | Surfaced as unmet demand in the all-hands | Help for customers who are not signed in today |
