# Detailed analysis

## 1. Was this a success?

Yes, on the evidence available. The project met its core goal of moving all in-app chat traffic to an agent Varo controls, and it did so in roughly four months of pilot to production work. The measurable gains are real: card resolution without a person rose 13 points in three months (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026), and the reported promoter score rose by about 30 or more points (project lead reporting).

## 2. What made it work

* **A clear business deadline.** The busy season forced focus and trade-offs (Customer service partner next steps, Nov 12, 2025).
* **Choosing control over convenience.** Owning workflows and content let Varo fix problems in days, not in a partner's release cycle.
* **Small, safe steps.** The 100-chat review, top-three fixes, and step by step rollout kept risk low (App Launch and Feedback Review, Mar 10, 2026).
* **Measurement built in from the start.** The data pipeline and dashboards were planned in January, not after launch (Data pipeline planning, Jan 14, 2026).

## 3. Where the story needs care

* **The promoter score needs its own proof.** It comes from my own reporting, and the survey report is held internally. When this goes to finance or leadership, the report should travel with the number.
* **The early dip.** Decagon started below Lex. This is a strength of the story if told upfront: the team chose to launch safely with a narrow scope, then widened it.
* **Cost savings are not yet shown.** The case today rests on customer experience and resolution. A before and after on human agent hours and cost per chat would complete it for finance.

## 4. What each audience cares about

| Audience | What to lead with |
|---|---|
| Leadership | Full production in about four months; promoter score up; clear third quarter plan |
| Support operations | More card questions solved without an agent; better handoffs; agents focus on harder cases |
| Product | Top contact reasons now have a path to self-service; card, disputes, and login next |
| Engineering | Secure account connections; lean testing; lessons on vendor data changes |
| Compliance and legal | Privacy masking; automatic risk monitors; fixed handoff rules for sensitive topics |
| Finance | Cost fit an approved budget; 40% dispute cost target; savings measurement still to come |

## 5. Recommended next actions

1. Publish the promoter score dashboard with dates, response counts, and method.
2. Measure human agent hours and cost per chat before and after.
3. Write down rollout pause rules and an incident process for the live system.
4. Build the repeat contact check to catch wrong answers.
5. Report card resolution again after card actions go live, to show the next step up.
