# Launch and gradual rollout

## The rule: never switch everyone at once

The plan from January was to move customers over in steps: staff and friends first, then 10%, then 30%, and onward (Decagon call, Jan 20, 2026). The March plan started at 5% (App Launch and Feedback Review, Mar 10, 2026).

## The launch loop

From the March 10 launch review (App Launch and Feedback Review, Mar 10, 2026):

1. Wait for Apple to approve the app update that turns on the new chat.
2. Collect the first 100 real conversations (expected to take one to two days).
3. Put them in a shared sheet. Each team marks problems and how serious they are.
4. Pick the top three critical issues and fix them.
5. Rerun the standard test questions to make sure nothing else broke.
6. Open to 5% of customers, then keep growing.

## Early read (April 3, 2026)

Decagon resolved 43% of chats over the prior seven days, compared with 55% for Lex. The team expected this gap, because Decagon was only answering from help articles at that point. Account actions such as closing an account and managing a card were days away (Decagon company training, Apr 3, 2026).

## Full production

By July 8, 2026, Decagon handled 100% of chat traffic (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026).
