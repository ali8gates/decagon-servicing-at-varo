# Executive summary

## What we did

Varo moved its in-app customer support chat from Amazon Lex, a scripted bot, to Decagon, a support assistant that reads what the customer actually wrote, looks up the right help content, and can take simple actions on the account. I led the work from pilot to production, partnering with the customer experience product manager from day one.

## Why it mattered

* Customers had to fit their questions into fixed scripts. When the script failed, they waited for a person (Decagon company training, Apr 3, 2026).
* January through March is Varo's busiest support season, so the team needed the new assistant ready in the first quarter of 2026 (Customer service partner next steps, Nov 12, 2025).
* Varo wanted to own its data and its workflows, not rent a closed system (Customer service partner next steps, Nov 12, 2025).

## Results

* **Card questions resolved without a person:** 44% in April 2026, 57% in July 2026 (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026).
* **Net promoter score:** about 40 to 50 before, over 80 after, based on my own reporting. See the [results page](08-results-and-promoter-score.md) for how to present this safely.
* **Next savings target:** about 40% lower cost to take in customer disputes once that workflow moves to Decagon (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026).

## What is next

Card actions (activate and freeze), dispute intake, and help for customers who cannot log in are the top three priorities for the third quarter of 2026 (Weekly team sync, data product, Jul 8, 2026).
